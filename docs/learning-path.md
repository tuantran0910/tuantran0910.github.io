Study the lessons in order or jump to a topic. All 19 lessons were rewritten and expanded from earlier material. They are **not complete verbatim transcripts**. See each lesson's source note and the [content audit](./reference/content-audit.md) for verification status.

01. **[Lesson 01: Argo CD Internal Architecture & Reconciliation Mechanism](./01-foundations/lesson-01-foundations.md)** — Argo CD internals, GitOps desired state, application controller, repo server, live-state cache, sync, hooks, and drift.
02. **[Lesson 02: Kubernetes Server-Side Apply, Field Ownership & Conflict Resolution](./01-foundations/lesson-02-core-resources.md)** — Server-Side Apply, managedFields, field managers, three-way merge, and conflicts.
03. **[Lesson 03: Structured Merge Diff, OpenAPI Schema & Field Ownership](./01-foundations/lesson-03-cluster-architecture.md)** — Structured Merge Diff, Kubernetes schemas, list semantics, and field ownership.
04. **[Lesson 04: Argo CD ApplicationSet & Multi-Cluster GitOps](./02-controllers-platform/lesson-04-controller-foundations.md)** — ApplicationSet generators, templating, multi-cluster deployment, and bootstrap.
05. **[Lesson 05: Config Connector, Config Sync & Cloud Resource Reconciliation](./02-controllers-platform/lesson-05-platform-integration.md)** — Google Cloud infrastructure CRDs, Config Sync, and controller ownership.
06. **[Lesson 06: GKE Workload Identity Federation & External Secrets](./02-controllers-platform/lesson-06-workload-identity-secrets.md)** — Workload Identity Federation, IAM, ESO, and Secret Manager.
07. **[Lesson 07: Kubernetes Admission Controllers & Policy](./02-controllers-platform/lesson-07-admission.md)** — Mutating and validating admission, webhooks, and CEL policies.
08. **[Lesson 08: Kubernetes Operator Internals & Controller Runtime](./02-controllers-platform/lesson-08-operators.md)** — CRDs, reconciliation, controller-runtime, finalizers, and conditions.
09. **[Lesson 09: Infrastructure GitOps & Platform Bootstrap](./03-gitops-delivery/lesson-09-gitops-bootstrap.md)** — Terraform, Argo CD App-of-Apps, ApplicationSet, and controller ownership.
10. **[Lesson 10: Production Reliability & Progressive Delivery](./03-gitops-delivery/lesson-10-progressive-delivery.md)** — RollingUpdate, canary releases, blue-green deployments, Argo Rollouts, and AnalysisTemplate.
11. **[Lesson 11: Kubernetes Observability & SRE Fundamentals](./04-observability/lesson-11-observability.md)** — Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, RED, USE, SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets.
12. **[Lesson 12: Kubernetes Networking Internals](./05-networking/lesson-12-kubernetes-networking.md)** — CNI, Service, EndpointSlice, kube-proxy/eBPF, DNS, and NetworkPolicy.
13. **[Lesson 13: GKE Networking & Cloud Load Balancing](./05-networking/lesson-13-gke-networking.md)** — Alias IPs, NEGs, GKE Gateway, Cloud NAT, and private clusters.
14. **[Lesson 14: Service Mesh & Istio Internals](./05-networking/lesson-14-istio.md)** — Envoy, istiod, xDS, mTLS, VirtualService, and Ambient Mesh.
15. **[Lesson 15: Kubernetes Scheduling & Autoscaling](./06-compute-storage/lesson-15-scheduling.md)** — Scheduler Filter and Score, requests and limits, HPA, VPA, and GKE autoscaling.
16. **[Lesson 16: Kubernetes Storage & Stateful Workloads](./06-compute-storage/lesson-16-storage.md)** — PVC, PV, StorageClass, CSI, StatefulSet, GKE storage, and snapshots.
17. **[Lesson 17: High Availability & Disaster Recovery](./07-reliability/lesson-17-disaster-recovery.md)** — Node and zone failures, regional GKE, RPO/RTO, backup, and failover.
18. **[Lesson 18: Kubernetes Security & Supply Chain](./08-security-governance/lesson-18-security.md)** — Pod Security, RBAC, WIF, SBOM, SLSA, signatures, and admission.
19. **[Lesson 19: Multi-Tenancy & Platform Governance](./08-security-governance/lesson-19-multi-tenancy.md)** — RBAC, quotas, NetworkPolicy, AppProject, and self-service tenants.
